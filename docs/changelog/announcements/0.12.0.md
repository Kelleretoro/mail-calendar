# Allodia Mail & Calendar 0.12.0

Released 13 October 2026.

This release carries 54 changes, and two of them are what it is about. Settings → Accounts now lists every account on every app, mail or not, each with a page of its own to choose what it is used for, link it to another account, sign it in again or change its servers. Linking lets mail from one provider sit beside a calendar and contacts from another, such as IMAP and SMTP at one host with CalDAV and CardDAV at a second, and setting up an account offers the same choice. And on Yahoo and mail servers like it, marking a message read, unread or flagged now shows in the app, where before the change was lost because the server spells the Inbox's name differently; large Yahoo folders also show every message and sync faster. Beside those: headings, alignment and fonts in the composer, pasted text that keeps its formatting, and drafts that reopen as they were written, including ones started in Thunderbird or Outlook.

Changes are grouped by where they landed. The first section reached **every app**. The sections after it cover changes that did not reach all of them, so a change that landed on two apps is listed under both.

---

## 🌟 Every app: macOS, iPhone & iPad, Windows, Android and Linux

### ✨ New

- **A draft reopens as it was written.** A draft you open again, or a message you edit from the Outbox, now keeps its formatting, pictures, quoted message and signature.
- **Headings, alignment and fonts in the composer, and drafts from Thunderbird and Outlook.** The composer adds headings, alignment, indentation, horizontal lines and fonts, and drafts started in Thunderbird or Outlook open with their formatting, quote and signature.
- **Pasting keeps formatting.** Text pasted into a message keeps its bold, italics, underlining, links, lists and tables.
- **Recognise a Microsoft 365 domain by its Autodiscover record.** An address at a domain whose mail is hosted by Microsoft 365 is now recognised as such, even when an older mail host still publishes settings for it.

### 🔧 Fixed

- **A Yahoo folder shows every message within the sync depth.** On a Yahoo account, a folder with more than 1000 messages within the sync depth now shows all of them, not only the newest 1000.
- **A draft in a conversation says it is a draft.** In a conversation, a draft is now marked Draft instead of Sent, and opening a conversation in Drafts opens its latest draft for editing.
- **A preview without the stylesheet.** The message list no longer shows a message's formatting code instead of its first lines.
- **A reopened draft keeps its signature.** A draft you open again keeps its signature, also when you change the sender, and a reopened reply can switch quote style.
- **Drafts save in Outlook, Gmail and signed-in Fastmail accounts.** Drafts now save in Outlook and Gmail accounts, and in Fastmail accounts added by signing in.
- **Fetch the calendar and contacts after signing an account in again, and a new account's contacts.** A calendar or contacts added by signing an account in again now appear straight away, as do a new account's contacts.
- **Find a calendar server again when the provider moves it.** When a calendar or address-book server stops answering, the app looks it up again and moves to the new address if your provider moved it.
- **Keep a first JMAP sync whole while another device changes the account.** A first sync of a JMAP account no longer misses messages when another device changes the mailbox while it runs.
- **Lines reach recipients spaced as they were written.** Lines in a message now reach recipients, Outlook included, spaced as they were in the composer.
- **Offer Fastmail sign-in again.** Adding a Fastmail account offers to sign in with Fastmail again, instead of asking for an app password.
- **Read and flag changes show at once; a write the server cannot take yet is kept.** Marking a message as read or flagged now shows straight away, and an action the server cannot take right now, offline or rate limited, is kept and sent later instead of being undone.
- **Read and flag changes show on Yahoo and similar servers.** Marking a message as read, unread or flagged on a Yahoo account, or on a mail server like it, now shows in the app, and a message moved elsewhere no longer stays in its old folder.
- **Saved replies stay in their conversation.** A reply or forward saved as a draft now stays in its conversation, and a reply started from the message list quotes the original.
- **Say a refused sign-in in words, not as the server's reply.** A wrong password or token now says the server didn’t accept the sign-in, instead of showing the server’s technical reply.
- **Spaces and tabs reach recipients as typed.** Extra spaces, indentation and tabs in a message now reach recipients as you typed them.
- **Sync large JMAP calendars and address books.** A JMAP calendar or address book with more entries than its server hands over in one go now syncs, such as a large Fastmail calendar.
- **The first sync of an IMAP folder is faster, most of all on Yahoo.** The first sync of a mail folder is faster, especially for Yahoo accounts.
- **The window stays responsive while the app opens its mailbox.** The app no longer freezes while it opens your mailbox after an update; it shows that it is opening it instead.
- **Thunderbird's colours and fonts show when reading, and Outlook's signature reads as text.** Colours, sizes and fonts from Thunderbird now show when you read a message, and an Outlook signature no longer looks like a link.
- **Unchanged Yahoo folders sync faster; IMAP accounts re-sync once.** Yahoo folders without changes now sync faster, and after updating, IMAP accounts download their message list once more.

## 🖥️ macOS

### ✨ New

- **Choose mail, calendar and contacts when setting an account up on Mac, iPhone and iPad.** When you add an account, you choose whether it is used for mail, calendar and contacts, and a Microsoft or Google sign-in asks only for those.
- **Every account in Settings → Accounts on Mac, iPhone and iPad, each with a page of its own.** Settings → Accounts lists every account, mail or not, each with a page to choose its uses, link it to another account, sign it in again or change its servers.
- **Link a new account to another while setting it up on Mac, iPhone and iPad.** Setting up an IMAP or CalDAV account offers to use another account's calendar or contacts with it.

### 🔧 Fixed

- **A closed composer releases its memory.** Closing a message you were writing now frees the memory it used, rather than holding it until the app quits.
- **Images over plain http load.** Images linked over plain http, common in email signatures, now load when you choose to load a message's images.
- **Mail links and shares open in the window already open.** A mail link or a shared file now opens its message in the window you already have open, rather than in a new one.
- **Say in Settings when a JMAP server does not offer a use, on Mac, iPhone, iPad and Windows.** Settings shows when a JMAP server does not offer calendar or contacts, rather than showing them as on.

## 🐧 Linux

### ✨ New

- **A suggested link on Linux.** An account's page in Settings → Accounts suggests the calendar that belongs to the same address, and links it with one click.
- **Choose mail, calendar and contacts when setting an account up on Linux.** When you add an account on Linux, you choose whether it is used for mail, calendar and contacts, so an account can also hold just a calendar and contacts.
- **Choose what a JMAP account is used for after signing in on Linux.** After you sign in to a JMAP account on Linux, you choose which of the mail, calendar and contacts its server offers you want to use, and Settings shows what the server does not offer.
- **Choose what a Microsoft or Google account is used for before signing in on Linux.** On Linux, a Microsoft or Google sign-in asks only for what you choose to use the account for, and says what the provider did not allow.
- **Ctrl+Shift+V pastes plain text on Linux.** Ctrl+Shift+V pastes text into a message without its formatting.
- **Edit an account's servers and password in Settings → Accounts on Linux.** Settings → Accounts can now change an account's servers and password, trying them before anything is saved.
- **Link a new account to another while setting it up on Linux.** On Linux, setting up an IMAP or CalDAV account offers to use another account's calendar or contacts with it.
- **Set up a calendar and contacts server by hand on Linux.** On Linux, the manual setup form adds an address book field and a type for calendar and contacts servers without mail.
- **Signing a Microsoft or Google account in again keeps it on Linux.** Signing a Microsoft or Google account in again keeps its mail and settings, and Settings → Accounts can now ask the provider for a use it has not allowed.
- **Use a calendar from another account with a JMAP account on Linux.** On Linux, a JMAP account whose server has no calendar can use one from another account, and setup offers the calendar server it found for your address.

### 🔧 Fixed

- **A personal Microsoft or Google account no longer offers colleagues on Linux.** A personal Microsoft or Google account no longer offers Colleagues in Settings → Accounts, since it has no organisation directory.
- **Add accounts from Settings on Linux.** On Linux, you add another account from Settings → Accounts, where your accounts are also changed and removed, rather than from a button above the folders.
- **Keep account setup in front of Settings on Linux.** On Linux, the window for adding an account stays in front of Settings when you come back to the app.
- **New Mail starts empty on Linux.** New Mail now opens an empty message, even with another message open beside it.
- **Open the app normally on Linux when the only account is a calendar.** On Linux, the app no longer opens on account setup when your only account is used for a calendar and contacts, and removing your last account takes you back to setup.
- **Settings on Linux closes with its close button alone.** The Settings window on Linux has one close button instead of a Done button beside it.
- **Settings opens while composing on Linux.** Settings now opens while you are writing a message, and the message stays as it was.
- **Settings → Accounts keeps its place on Linux.** Settings → Accounts keeps its scroll position after a change, and shows a suggested link as soon as the server names it.
